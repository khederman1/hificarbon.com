// About

A 21st century solution to a 20th century problem.

HiFi Carbon wants to change the carbon conversation. Founded in 2025 by a leading researcher and an over-caffeinated engineer, HiFi Carbon has developed a low-cost, industrial-grade unit that is simple to operate and can be easily retrofitted on existing sites.

HiFi Carbon believes this solution will allow for a shared vision of the future of energy — a new technical lens that will allow us to leverage existing infrastructure, lower costs, and facilitate the development of new, valuable industries.

// Team

Built by those who've done it before.

Kevin Hederman
CEO & Co-Founder

Engineer and technical generalist. Over 2 decades of experience working on upgrading today's energy systems to supply tomorrow's energy demands. Design engineer (Houston), construction engineer (Pennsylvania), operations manager (Boston), and organization builder (2012 - present).

Dr. Joshua Dean
CTO & Co-Founder

Research Professor and scientist with over 15 years experience leading international research consortia to trace global carbon cycles. Philip Leverhulme Prize winner featured on the cover of the leading scientific journal Nature, UK Research & Innovation Future Leaders Fellow, Principal Investigator (University of Bristol, since 2022), permanent member of faculty (University of Liverpool, 2018-2022).

Chang Hong
General Counsel

Extensive experience in early-stage biotech and sustainability companies. Over 2 decades as in-house counsel for renewable fuel, biotech, pharma, and high-tech companies, both public and private. Hands-on experience and a deep understanding of legal, R&D, operations, and commercialization activities necessary to execute on the vision.
